Full description

Our pediatric and gynaecology services located on the Jura Hospital site in Delémont and in MediPed, accompany women, children and their families throughout their lives. Our multidisciplinary teams are committed every day to providing benevolent and personalized assistance in an appropriate and reassuring environment. Warm hospitality, effective consultation and rigorous follow-up are essential to ensure optimal patient care. In order to complete our paediatric team, located at our sites in Delémont and Porrentruy , and with a view to the next opening of MediPed SA in Porrentruy , we are looking for an entry into office or to be agreed upon :

Responsibilities

  • Provide patient reception and telephone call management.
  • Organize appointment and administrative follow-up of medical records.
  • Write and process medical correspondence related to service activity.
  • Work closely with paediatricians to ensure optimal patient care.
  • Attending certain paediatric consultations: installing patients, taking parameters, preparing vaccines, taking blood.

Qualifications

  • Diploma of medical assistant or equivalent.
  • Essential experience in paediatrics.
  • Mastery of computer tools (Word, Excel, Powerpoint, Outlook). Knowledge of software SIC RAP and Pulse Medica an advantage.
  • Mastery of the French language and medical terminology.
  • Acute sense of organization, adaptability and autonomy.
  • Of a calm and attentive nature, you express yourself eloquently.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to manage sensitive situations with empathy and professionalism.
  • Flexibility at Delémont and Porrentruy sites.

Benefits

  • Quick decision-making paths with a positive work culture that promotes open and respectful communication.
  • A stimulating working environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
