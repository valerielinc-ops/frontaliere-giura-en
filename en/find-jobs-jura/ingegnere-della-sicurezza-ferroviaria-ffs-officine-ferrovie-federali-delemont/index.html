What awaits you.

Come on board, to the first-class employer! An innovative work environment awaits you, with many motivated and motivating colleagues. Continuing training is not neglected for your individual professional development. We offer many benefits - convince yourself: Social benefits. What you can make move.

  • You build and maintain various safety installations ensuring rail traffic such as railway signaling, control systems, track clearance monitoring systems, ETCS beacons, level crossings, etc.
  • You commission and decommission security installations.
  • You analyze and resolve disturbances at the installations.
  • After internal training, you will provide on-call service (home location near Delémont, accessible in 30 minutes).

What you bring.

  • You hold a CFC in automobile mechanic, agricultural machinery, polymechanic, locksmith or a profession deemed equivalent.
  • You are willing to follow internal training as a security installation technician as well as regular continuing training.
  • You have a strong sense of responsibility and high sensitivity to security issues.
  • You have a category B driving license.
