Full description

Reference code: JR136303

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Within our Maintenance team, you play a key role in the availability and performance of our production equipment. Polyvalent, reactive and solutions-oriented, you ensure preventive and corrective maintenance while actively contributing to the reliability and continuous improvement of our production tool.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Preventive maintenance

  • Design, implement and maintain equipment maintenance plans.
  • Conduct preventive maintenance interventions, including operations requiring special technical expertise.
  • Assist operators in conducting routine interviews and provide them with the necessary support.

Corrective maintenance

  • Provide technical support to production teams.
  • Diagnosing malfunctions and performing the necessary interventions to minimize downtime of equipment.
  • Ensure traceability of interventions and maintain information in the GMAO.

Installation & monitoring of equipment

  • Participate in the installation and commissioning of new equipment.
  • Support external stakeholders and ensure that work is carried out in accordance with defined requirements.
  • Follow-up of external interventions and carry out the necessary reporting to the responsible person.

Continuous improvement & reliability

  • Contribute to the improvement of production equipment by adding or modifying existing devices.
  • Identify and implement solutions to improve machine reliability and availability.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

With technical training in maintenance, electrotechnical or equivalent, you have solid knowledge in mechanics, pneumatics, hydraulics and electricity and automation. You are comfortable with reading drawings, diagrams and technical instructions and ideally have a good knowledge of machining centers or automated systems.

You have skills in cold and refrigeration techniques, ideally attested by a degree in cold and/or certification in this field.

You master the usual computer tools as well as Computer Assisted Maintenance Management (GMAO) software. Knowledge of health as well as English or German technical skills are also assets.

Your autonomy, analytical capacity, responsiveness and sense of service allow you to intervene effectively with production teams and contribute sustainably to the reliability of our equipment.

WHAT WE HAVE TO OFFER YOU?

We offer flexible working hours in a varied and stimulating environment. We benefit from the benefits of the collective labour agreement for the Swiss watch and microtechnic industries, including 5 weeks of holidays per year, plus additional leave related to catching up.

Due to our environmental impact, we focus on soft mobility and public transport with financial participation. Our restaurant offers fresh and varied meals every day at a great price. Oriented towards well-being at work, our event committee offers many cultural and sporting activities throughout the year.

In addition, the Richemont Group has been certified Equal-Pay, which ensures fair remuneration for all our employees. Not to mention our pension fund, which offers good benefits to enable everyone to ensure their financial future related to social benefits.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity.
  • We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity.
  • We take care of the world we live in.

YOUR TRAVEL WITH US

If your profile matches our search, you will be contacted for an exploratory call. Subsequently, you will be offered a first interview and then a second interview with other hierarchical stakeholders.
