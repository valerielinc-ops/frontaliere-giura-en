Full description

Our therapy units are composed of professionals specialized in the rehabilitation and promotion of the movement. We support patients in their recovery and autonomy through appropriate therapeutic approaches.

In order to strengthen our team on our Porrentruy site , we are looking for an entry into office from 1 January 2027 or to agree , one·e :

Responsibilities

  • Evaluate the physical capacities of patients and develop appropriate physical activity programmes to suit their needs.
  • Provide supervision and facilitation of individual or group sessions.
  • Work closely with the interdisciplinary team (physiotherapists, doctors, occupational therapists, etc.) to optimize patient care.
  • Monitor patient progression and adjust procedures accordingly.
  • Participate in the promotion of physical activity as a therapeutic tool.

Qualifications

  • Master in Adapted Physical Activity (APA).
  • Experience in a medical or rehabilitation environment.
  • Excellent relational skills, listening skills and a sense of pedagogy.
  • Ability to adapt interventions to the needs and pathologies of patients.
  • Dynamism, autonomy and sense of initiative.

Benefits

  • Rapid decision-making pathways with a positive work culture that promotes open and respectful communication.
  • A stimulating working environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • Easy access to accommodation (furnished rooms and studios).
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
