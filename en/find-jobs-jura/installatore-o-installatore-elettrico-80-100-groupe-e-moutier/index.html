JOIN AN ENTERPRISES WHERE KNOW-WORK AND SPIRIT EVERY DAY

At Christian Kropf SA , we put our passion and expertise at the service of various projects – electrical installations, telephony, automation, lighting – combining tradition and new technologies. Whether they are family homes, businesses, industries or medical offices, we are committed to providing tailored, reliable and sustainable solutions.

Working at home means joining a team recognized for its experience, competence and flexibility. It is also evolving in a company that places quality, safety and listening at the heart of its business, while valuing the collaboration between the various trades and field monitoring.

To complement our team, we are currently looking for

Installer or Electrical Installer 80-100%

Mission

  • Performing work related to multi-technical electrical installations, you master the reading of plans and diagrams of electrical installations
  • Install, connect and modify electrical panels, various electrical appliances, communication, multimedia, security, control, etc...
  • Participate in various implementations
  • Making films and governance bills
  • Participation in the training of our apprentices

Profile

  • CFC installation plant
  • Confirmed experience in general electrical installations
  • Organized and autonomous, you have a sense of anticipation, responsibility and customer service
  • You are recognized as an open minded, flexible and team-minded person.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
