Full description

In order to strengthen its management, our dynamic Regional Bank, with more than 45 collaborators, is looking for an experienced personality, consulting, development and distribution.

Aufgaben

  • Actively contribute to the future of our Bank by participating in the implementation of the strategy defined.
  • Framework, federate and develop employees by fostering an environment of trust, commitment and sustainable performance.
  • Take responsibility for a sector of the Front Office and ensure its development and achievement.
  • Provide expertise and leadership through interdisciplinary projects (Bank and Raiffeisen Group).
  • Representing the Bank with regional stakeholders and helping to strengthen its local anchor and visibility.
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and appropriate risk management.

Anforderungen

  • Recognized higher education in banking, finance or economics (University, High School or equivalent), supplemented by at least 5 years of experience in a Front Office function in a Swiss bank.
  • Experience confirmed in customer advice, business development and team management.
  • Business vision, strong business sense and excellent understanding of customer needs.
  • Experience in project management and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Relationship strength, strong regional network and good knowledge of the local economic fabric.

Wir bieten

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.
