This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a Spare Parts Specialist, you monitor inventories, ensure the availability and quality of the parts and document all movements precisely. You support repairs, coordinate with technology, purchasing and maintenance and ensure that spare parts management runs smoothly.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Hauptlager Wohlen, Industriestrasse 21, 5610 Wohlen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You monitor and check the spare parts warehouse, conduct regular inventory checks and ensure that all parts are available and in perfect condition
  • Quality control is also one of your tasks – only parts with the highest quality are used
  • Recording, documentation and reports: You maintain the system, document warehouse movements and create comprehensible evaluations
  • During repairs, you assemble parts, select them on the ramp and also handle physical transport.
  • Communication with technology, purchasing and maintenance is central to optimally coordinate processes around spare parts

Requirements

  • First experience in the logistics environment: and spare parts
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EBA): Logistics
  • Completed training or further education in the field of logistics is an advantage – it is crucial that storage processes and spare parts are not foreign to you
  • In-depth knowledge of warehouse management systems and ERP tools (e.g. IFS Ultimo), including safe handling of Excel and the office package as well as high IT affinity
  • Technical understanding and analytical skills to use spare parts in a targeted manner, to evaluate stock data and to identify needs at an early stage
  • Efficient time and self-management while taking care of inventory control, quality control and documentation
  • Strong communication skills in German (English of advantage) as well as the ability to cooperate with technology, purchasing and maintenance across departments
  • High sense of responsibility and solution-oriented thinking, especially in the event of bottlenecks or problems in the storage process
  • Mobility for our two-shift operation (Mo–Fr 05:00–23:00 / Sa 05:00–18:00) in weekly change
  • Identify potential for improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • in German (negotiation certain)
