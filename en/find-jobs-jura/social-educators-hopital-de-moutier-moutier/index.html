Full description

Responsibilities Social educators are responsible for accompanying young people in vulnerable situations in a specialized care facility, with emphasis on promoting their general well-being, autonomy and social integration. Their main responsibilities are:

  • Actively engage in the day-to-day lives of young people and implement evidence-based educational and therapeutic programs in accordance with institutional standards. - Collaborate with an interdisciplinary team to ensure coordinated and effective service delivery. - Supervise the implementation and facilitation of structured therapeutic activities, including psychodrama, escalation and circus-inspired interventions.
  • To maintain professional relations with families and outside professionals in order to promote comprehensive care. - Establish and apply eligibility criteria for participation in programmes and systematically monitor the progress of participants. - Prepare comprehensive reports, maintain detailed individual records, and prepare clinical documentation in accordance with establishment protocols.
  • Respect scrupulously all institutional procedures and professional standards governing the provision of care and support services. - Contribute to the strategic development and continuous improvement of unit operations and service delivery. - Conduct risk assessments and implement protective measures to ensure the safety and well-being of all school youth.
  • Participate in crisis response and implement de-escalation techniques where necessary, maintaining a calm and caring attitude. - Engage in reflexive practice and clinical supervision to improve professional skills and service quality. - Contribute to the collection and analysis of results data to assess the effectiveness of the program and to guide service improvement.
  • Social education diploma ES or HES.
  • Experience in psychiatry (5 years) and/or child psychiatry (an asset).
  • Interest in group therapeutic work.
  • Ability to work in a demanding institutional setting.
  • Professional skills: analysis, writing, interdisciplinary.
  • Relationship skills: listening, communication, collaboration.
  • Personal skills: adaptation, ethics, sense of responsibility.
  • A varied activity within a team of a changing sector
  • Pay and motivating working conditions

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Community-based and multi-disciplinary institutions serving patients, Moutier Hospital and Saint-Imier Hospital offer high value-added activities in the somatic and mental health fields. With nearly 1,200 employees, the hospitals, the Biel Medical Centre (CMB/MZB) and the Medicents of the Arc Network cover the medical-hospital needs of a population of more than 50,000 inhabitants and are characterised by their dynamism, excellent infrastructure and constant concern to develop services of proximity and quality. The Arc Network is also the first Swiss integrated care organization.
