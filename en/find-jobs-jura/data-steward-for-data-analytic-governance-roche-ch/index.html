Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities: Metadata Management & Stewardship
  • Ensure the accuracy, consistency, and completeness of business and technical metadata across systems, platforms in the Finance Data Domain.
  • Maintain and enhance metadata repositories (such as Collibra, SAP Datasphere) to support data discovery, lineage, and understanding.
  • Data & Analytics Governance Framework and Methodology Alignment
  • Support the implementation and adherence to Data & Analytics Governance Framework.
  • Act as a liaison between business and IT teams to ensure metadata practices align with DAG Metatadata Methodology. Metadata Cataloging & Standardization
  • Collaborate with data product owners, report owners, data owners and subject matter experts to define, document, and manage metadata catalog.
  • Promote consistency and standardization of business terms and reference data definitions across the Finance Data Domain. Data Lineage & Impact Analysis
  • Establish and maintain end - to - end data lineage to enable traceability, impact assessment, and regulatory compliance.
  • Identify and resolve metadata gaps that may hinder data quality, trust, or decision-making. Stakeholder Engagement & Enablement

Application process

  • The Data and Analytics Governance (DAG) Data Steward is a key role responsible for ensuring the quality, integrity, and proper usage of data, and enabling effective data & analytics governance practices for Roche’s Digital Backbone; working closely with cross-functional teams in ASPIRE program, Roche Data Insights (RDI) Initiative, Informatics, Enterprise Master Data Management (EMDM) to ensure the consistent application of DAG policies, standards, and controls.
