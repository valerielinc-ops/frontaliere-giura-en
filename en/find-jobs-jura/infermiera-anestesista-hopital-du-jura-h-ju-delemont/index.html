Full description

The “Parents – woman – child” sector of the Jura Hospital, located on the Delémont site, brings together the Maternity and Pediatrics services. The Pediatrics department cares for newborns, infants, children and adolescents, from birth to 16 years, in several complementary sectors: neonatology, general ambulatory and inpatient pediatrics and pediatric emergencies.

In order to complete the Pediatrics team, we are looking for a replacement and entry into office from October 19, 2026, for a:

Responsibilities

  • · Provide care for neonatology, pediatric emergencies, and general pediatric inpatient and outpatient units, including child psychiatry.
  • Support families and those close to patients, ensuring their well-being.
  • · Coordinate patient care with various healthcare professionals.
  • · Assess the degree of urgency of the situation and provide quality, complex care, in an autonomous and medico-delegated manner.
  • · Ensure the continuity of information and the accuracy of transmissions.

Qualifications

  • · Bachelor in Nursing or title deemed equivalent.
  • · Recognition of the Red Cross for foreign diplomas (or to be requested).
  • · Experience of several years in an acute environment.
  • · Experience in Pediatrics would be a real asset.
  • · Good analytical and decision-making skills.
  • Excellent organizational skills, sense of priorities and initiative.
  • · Dynamic personality able to work independently.
  • · An ability to work in a multidisciplinary team.

Advantages

  • · Position within a multidisciplinary, dynamic and united team which works in a pleasant working climate.
  • · Fast decision-making pathways with a positive work culture promoting open and respectful communication.
