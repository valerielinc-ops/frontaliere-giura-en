Description

Do you have the passion for investment, customer experience and acquisition? Do you wish to have a concrete and measurable impact? Join our team to accelerate growth and customer excellence in Romandie! As a Mandate Specialist, you are at the heart of the UBS strategy by:

  • Actively advising clients and Client Advisors on discretionary and advisory investment solutions, covering all asset classes and markets
  • playing a leading role in the conclusion of new financial management mandates and customer loyalty, aiming at ambitious growth targets and maximizing customer coverage
  • organising and implementing a high volume of customer meetings, guaranteeing a personalized, relevant and impeccable experience
  • Working closely with the Chief Investment Office (CIO), heritage planning specialists and all stakeholders to deliver global, innovative and digital investment solutions
  • contributing to the dissemination of best practices, the expansion of the coverage of the Client Advisors population and the collective success of the team thanks to your commitment.
