Full description

Le Noirmont Clinic is a national centre for cardiovascular rehabilitation, internal medicine, oncology and psychosomatics, located in the Swiss Jura. Position: Assistant Cardiology Physician, Head of Clinic (F/H). Le Noirmont Clinic, located in the heart of an intact and sunny nature, is a national reference centre for cardiovascular rehabilitation, internal medicine, oncological, psychosomatic and musculoskeletal. The medical team supports post-operative and acute care patients in its personalized and multidisciplinary rehabilitation programs. The clinic has 92 beds, employs between 180 and 200 staff and receives more than 1500 patients per year, as well as outpatient patients. The team consists of two chief doctors, three assistant doctors, two chief clinicians, four assistants and is supported by a proactive and motivated medical secretariat. In order to complete our medical team, we are looking for a follow-up or suitable entry: Assistant Medical Officer or Head of Cardiology Clinic (80-100%) Activities Supervision of assistant doctors

  • Management of patients in outpatient cardiovascular rehabilitation
  • Active participation in the institution's projects, including the training programme
  • Participation in weekend and week shift
  • Monitoring of administrative tasks. Desired profile You have a federal degree in medicine or a degree recognized by MEBEKO; You have the title of Cardiology Specialist (FMH title) or equivalent (by need for head of clinic). An initial experience in Switzerland would be an asset
  • An advantage is that it is in possession of an SSUM certificate of further training (ethography, POCUS, ultrasonography); You are able to work in an interdisciplinary team and pass on your knowledge
  • You are French or German mother tongue and have a good knowledge of the other language (level B2 required), or have the will to improve it quickly.
  • We offer you A stimulating and interesting clinical activity within a human-sized and motivated team
  • The clinic has modern infrastructure, 4D echocardiography (incl. ETO, abdominal, vascular and musculoskeletal probes), pacemaker control, ECG Holter, blood pressure measurement (24h), oxymetry, conventional radiology and laboratory
  • Modern working conditions, training opportunities and good social benefits.
