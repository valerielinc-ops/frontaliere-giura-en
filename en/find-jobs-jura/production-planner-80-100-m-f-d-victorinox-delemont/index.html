General information Reference 2026-266 Position Description Position Title Production Planner 80%-100% m/f/d Mission Description The main objective of the position is to define and optimize the planning of the production of watch case shops, taking into account the various constraints and production data, in order to meet the needs of the watch manufacturing plan while controlling stock levels and avoiding overstocking. As such, the licensee shall continuously monitor the implementation of the production plan in close cooperation with the workshop managers, analyse any deviations and implement any corrective actions or planning adjustments necessary to ensure that production, quality and time objectives are met.
