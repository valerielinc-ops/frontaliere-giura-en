This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The radiology service of the Jura Hospital, distributed at the Delémont and Porrentruy sites, provides high quality diagnostic and interventional services thanks to a modern technical tray (IRM, scanner, ultrasound, conventional radiology, mammography). Our multidisciplinary team works closely with the various hospital services and family doctors to ensure prompt, reliable and tailored care for patients.

In order to complete our teams, we are looking for a suitable entry into office:

Responsibilities

  • Provide patient reception and telephone call management.
  • Organize appointments and planning for radiological examinations.
  • Write, format and process correspondence and medical reports.
  • Ensure administrative management of patient records and transmission of information to prescribers.
  • Collaborate with medical and healthcare teams to ensure smooth and high-quality management.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of the organisation of the medical secretariat.

Qualifications

  • Diploma of medical secretary or equivalent.
  • Experience in a medical service, ideally in radiology.
  • Mastery of computer tools (Word, Excel, Powerpoint, Outlook).
  • Mastery of the French language and medical terminology.
  • High organizational sensitivity, priority management and anticipation capacity.
  • Discretion, professionalism and excellent relational skills.
  • Ability to work as a team while being autonomous and rigorous.

Benefits

  • Quick decision-making paths with a positive work culture that promotes open and respectful communication.
  • A stimulating working environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
