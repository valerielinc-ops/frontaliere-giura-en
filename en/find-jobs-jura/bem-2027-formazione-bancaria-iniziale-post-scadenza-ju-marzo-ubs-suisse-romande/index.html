Role overview

Your profile Do you want to enter professional life directly after your maturity, assume responsibilities and discover the banking world in practice?

As a member of our Junior Talents, you start your career at UBS.

From the beginning, you are integrated into daily activities: you participate in processing customer or product-related requests, you analyse cases and you prepare interviews.

You discover different fields through semi-annual rotations that will allow you to quickly develop a global understanding of our activity.

What awaits us

  • a training of 18 months with various insights in our various sectors and agencies
  • three six-month rotations during which you work in customer consulting and product consulting (present and digital).

This will allow you to familiarize yourself with topics such as investments, credits, mortgages and payments

  • For each rotation, a practical training manager accompanies and supports you in your development
  • an excellent starting point in the banking sector, with real prospects for your future career Your training Your training is practical, varied and focused on your personal development.
