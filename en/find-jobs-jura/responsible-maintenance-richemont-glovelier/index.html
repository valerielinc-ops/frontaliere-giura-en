Each House proudly embodies a tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont strives to preserve the heritage and identity of each. WE RECOGNIZE

Integrated into the Technical Directorate at the Varinor site in Delémont, you will manage the building maintenance, maintenance and concierge sectors.

HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT?

  • Define maintenance policy
  • Manage maintenance budget
  • Propose actions to improve and reduce costs
  • Participate in industrial investment policy
  • Oversee maintenance of production facilities and buildings
  • Manager of the various maintenance / concierge services
  • Develop GMAO and leverage all its capabilities
  • Ensure the smooth running of technical actions related to production, safety, environment and working conditions
  • Negotiate, establish and monitor maintenance service contracts
  • Coordinate project implementation and monitoring

THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS? YOUR SKILLS!

As a result of advanced training in industrial maintenance, you must have a confirmed experience in a similar position. Expert in various techniques (electricity, mechanics, hydraulics, pneumatics, etc.), you have perfect mastery of GMAO (piloting and development) as well as Microsoft office and ideally SAP. Proactive, rigorous and flexible, you are oriented to results. Your sense of responsibility, organization and service will be as important to carry out your missions.
