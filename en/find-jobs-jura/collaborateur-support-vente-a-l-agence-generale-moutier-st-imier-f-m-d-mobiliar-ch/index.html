Full description

What awaits you

In this role, you actively support our insurance and foresight advisors in their daily activities. Through your sense of service and commitment, you contribute directly to providing our customers with professional, reliable and quality support.

What you can achieve with us

Your main mission is to provide effective support in the monitoring of contracts and in the processing of simple claims.

Thanks to your customer-oriented approach, you support our clients with attention and professionalism throughout their efforts, providing them with security and confidence.

What you bring

  • Insurance training, ideally supplemented by the AFA intermediate certificate
  • Sociable personality, comfortable in customer contact and motivated by accompaniment in case of a simple disaster
  • Category B driving licence
  • Initiative, autonomy and organizational sense
  • Responsibility, empathy and openness
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written. Mastering another language is an asset

Working at home

The Moutier-St-Imier General Agency offers you a versatile, stimulating and varied position in a young and motivated team. You benefit from modern infrastructure, attractive working conditions and continuing training opportunities. To perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act provides for the training of AFA insurance intermediary, non-life profiles and life. If you do not yet have this certificate, we will gladly accompany you in obtaining it by means of adapted internal trainings.

Entry into office shall be agreed as soon as possible. The main workplace is in St-Imier, with a few days a year at the Moutier site. The occupancy rate is negotiable from 80%. Below you will find more information about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Participation in the outcome

Our success also benefits our employees. Each year we decide on the payment of a contribution to the result and its amount.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and various discounts on cultural events.

Interested?: Send us your application file quickly and easily by email. We look forward to meeting you. Mr Christopher Cudré-Mauroux is happy to be at your disposal for any question.
