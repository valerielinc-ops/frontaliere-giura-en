Full description

Your future playground You will join the Delémont store, opened in June 2026, made up of a team of 6 collaborators. And now, you're looking for a dynamic job where no day looks alike? Do you want to share your passion for sport and offer an unforgettable experience to every customer who crosses the store doors? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? Then the position of Sales Advisor Rando is made for you. Why?

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the link.
  • You're the actor of tomorrow's business. You're not just selling a product on the shelf. You offer an omni-channel and lasting experience: digital orders, equipment rental, recovery of old products (Second Life)... You answer every need.
  • You have autonomy and impact. You guarantee your sport! You make sure your department makes you want, you manage your stock and you set up your business operations. Your efficiency can be seen directly on the satisfaction of your customers. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • Advice and omnichannel sale: You welcome your customers as guests. You advise them, you conclude sales (in physics or through our digital tools), you offer our loyalty program and you manage the cash flow.
  • Commercial Dynamism / Merchandising: You implement the products according to the seasons and logical implementation, you manage the "facing" so that your radius is always impeccable, and you highlight the best sales and new products of the season.
  • Availability and logistics: Put your hand to the dough! From receipt of the delivery to dispatch, you guarantee the accuracy of your stocks and the rapid availability of your products. You're also preparing web orders.
  • Circular economy and services: You are an ambassador of our new models: you manage the purchase of used equipment and you push our rental solutions to extend the life of our products.
  • Specific missions: Depending on your desires and your development, you can take reference missions on a specific subject, become a trainer for your colleagues, or even attend the shop's permanence. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in peace.
  • You don't like going spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and processing merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You're not ready to see 10,000 steps on your watch every night.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
