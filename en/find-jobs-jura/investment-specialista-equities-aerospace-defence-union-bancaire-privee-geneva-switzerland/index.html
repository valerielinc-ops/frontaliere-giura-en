Role overview

Mission

We are seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Equity Analyst to join the Investment Specialist team.

The successful candidate will be responsible for conducting in-depth financial analysis of publicly traded companies in specific sectors (Industrials, Aerospace & Defence), evaluating investment opportunities, building financial models, and providing actionable insights to support portfolio management and investment decisions to Wealth Management teams (DPM, Private Banking).

  • We are seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Equity Analyst to join the Investment Specialist team.

  • Conduct thorough fundamental research on assigned companies and sectors across US and Europe.

  • 5–10 years of experience covering the Industrials and Aerospace & Defence sectors, with a strong understanding of key value drivers and industry dynamics.

Main responsibilities

  • Main responsibilities Company and Industry Research
  • Conduct thorough fundamental research on assigned companies and sectors across US and Europe.
  • Analyze business models, industry dynamics, competitive positioning, and management quality.
  • Maintain ongoing coverage of ~40-50 companies (Buy, Hold, Sell ratings), updating investment theses and valuations as new data becomes available.
  • Build and maintain financial models forecasting key financial metrics, evaluate capital structure and costs as well as balance sheet health. Investment Recommendation and Reporting
  • Prepare investment reports (equity factsheets, updated quarterly), internal and external presentations summarizing sector views, valuation conclusions, and single line recommendations.
  • Support senior portfolio managers (DPM) in developing investment strategies and identifying high-conviction opportunities.
  • Communicate investment views clearly and concisely to internal stakeholders (private bankers, investment advisors) or clients through written reports, slide decks, or verbal presentations.
  • Market and Portfolio Monitoring & Management
  • Assist in portfolio monitoring, identifying risks or catalysts that could affect portfolio performance (DPM Equity portfolios).

Key requirements

  • Excellent academic record with a relevant Master's degree from a top-tier university and CFA
  • 5–10 years of experience covering the Industrials and Aerospace & Defence sectors, with a strong understanding of key value drivers and industry dynamics.
  • Strong ability to conduct company and industry research.
  • Strong analytical and financial modelling skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to present investment ideas clearly and confidently to internal stakeholders and Clients.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Proactive, self-motivated, and results-oriented, with the ability to work independently.
