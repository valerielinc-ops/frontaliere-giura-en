Full description

Our occupational therapy service, present on the Delémont and Porrentruy sites, supports patients with physical, cognitive or psychological limitations, temporary or permanent, in order to promote their autonomy and quality of life. Our teams work in a multidisciplinary environment, meeting the needs of each person as closely as possible.

In order to complete our team on the Delémont site, we are looking, for a start on January 1, 2027 or to be agreed, for a:

Responsibilities

  • · Assess patients’ functional, cognitive and psychosocial capacities and define personalized treatment goals.
  • · Implement therapeutic interventions aimed at restoring, maintaining or developing autonomy in activities of daily living.
  • · Advise patients and their loved ones on auxiliary means and adaptation of the environment, with a view to promoting reintegration into their living environment.
  • · Collaborate closely with the multidisciplinary team to ensure consistent follow-up of patients.
  • · Document interventions and ensure the transmission of relevant information.

Qualifications

  • · Bachelor in occupational therapy HES or title deemed equivalent.
  • · Recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas.
  • · Experience in a hospital or rehabilitation environment, an asset.
  • · Autonomy, sense of organization and ability to adapt.
  • · Empathy, interpersonal skills and sense of support.
  • · Be willing to travel occasionally to our Porrentruy site.

Benefits

  • · Fast decision-making pathways with a positive work culture promoting open and respectful communication.
  • · Stimulating work environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • · Easy access to accommodation (furnished rooms and studios).
  • · A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
