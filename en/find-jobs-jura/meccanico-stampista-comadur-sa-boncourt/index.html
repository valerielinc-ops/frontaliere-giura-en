Engineer - Moulist - Swatch Group

Engineer - Moulist Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of hard materials components Swiss Made. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of the Group's brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry.

  • As part of our growth and development, we offer a position on our Boncourt site:
  • MECANICIAN – MOULIST
  • You will need to ensure the installation, development, certification and maintenance of injection moulds according to safety and production requirements.
  • A modern and pleasant work space.
  • Social conditions of first order.
  • A dynamic, demanding team listening and always looking for new challenges.
  • A common will: to collaborate and grow together to become an indispensable partner.
  • Description of post
  • Ensure assembly of new moulds and maintenance of production moulds
  • Assemble, adjust, adjust, and check all functions of our injection moulds or tools
  • Make mussels meeting quality requirements available to production
  • Participate in the tests and perform the necessary corrections
  • Analyze mechanical malfunctions and propose appropriate corrective measures
  • Ensure cleaning, maintenance and repair of moulds

In addition, we rely on your experience to be a proposal force for process changes and/or improvements. We also expect you to be able to take charge of one-off general micromechanical activities for other sectors

Profile

  • Mechanic CFC, Polymechanic CFC or equivalent training
  • At least 5 years of experience
  • Control of conventional and measuring machines
  • Knowledge of injection or stamp would be a plus
  • Control of CNC machines would be a plus
  • Rigor, accuracy, analytical spirit and solutions orientation
  • Flexibility and availability
  • Qualifications
  • We are looking for an organized person, able to work in multitasking with excellent relational qualities both with external and internal interlocutors.
  • Equipped with a good open mind, creativity and dynamism, knowing how to step back and listen.
  • Languages
  • French
