Role overview

WOLF is a leading provider of sustainable heat pumps, hybrid solutions and highly efficient ventilation systems.

WOLF supports planners, installers and end customers in the entire design process and the coordination of the components of heating systems and ventilation units in the areas of heating, solar, air conditioning and ventilation and thus ensures an optimal indoor climate – for single-family houses and multi-family houses up to office buildings and industrial plants.

WOLF is part of the Ariston Group, a global leader in sustainable thermal comfort and offers a unique, comprehensive solution for climate comfort, hot water and air treatment as well as components and burners.
