Full description

The Banque Raiffeisen Franches-Montagnes, a cooperative company with a human dimension, dynamic and regional anchor, is seeking, in order to complete its team, one/one employee administration of the credits.

Aufgaben

  • Processing credit claims: data entry and control, drawing up and checking contractual documents, opening of accounts, drawing up of accounts, reimbursing and closing credit positions
  • Management of credit positions for private and corporate customers, data entry for balance sheet analysis, processing of construction credits and management of mortgage base data and guarantees
  • Support in credit counselling
  • Telephone contacts and written correspondence with authorities, insurance and other banks
  • Ability to work in a flexwork

Anforderungen

  • Banking experience in Switzerland and in the field of credit
  • Ability to integrate digital tools into processes
  • Good team spirit
  • Auto-Studies, flexibility, rigour and organization in work

Wir bieten: Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. They shape our way of working together and acting toward each other. The culture of tutoirement promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.
