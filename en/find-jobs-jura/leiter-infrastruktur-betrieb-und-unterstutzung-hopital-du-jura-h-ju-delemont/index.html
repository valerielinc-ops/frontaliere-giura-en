Full description

The IT Service of the Jura Hospital, attached to the Operational Management Directorate, ensures the day-to-day operation of the institution's information system. Its teams guarantee the availability and security of infrastructure as well as the quality of support to users.

As part of the evolution of the organization of our IT Service , we are looking for:

Responsibilities

  • · Drive the Support & Workstations and Infrastructure & Operations teams, developing the skills, autonomy and accountability of employees.
  • · Ensure availability, performance and security of infrastructure, and move operations towards a proactive and standardized approach.
  • · Defining and changing the technological base of the information system, anticipating future developments, obsolescence and needs.
  • · Ensure the quality of handling of user incidents and requests, and manage major incidents.
  • · Pilot external suppliers and partners, monitor compliance with their commitments and participate in budget monitoring.
  • · Designing, coordinating and piloting IT and cross-cutting projects in conjunction with business teams and external partners.

Qualifications

  • · Advanced training in computer science, management computing or related fields (Bachelor HES/University, ES degree or equivalent).
  • · Minimum 5 years of experience operating complex computer environments.
  • · Experience confirmed in team management and leadership, as well as in piloting external suppliers and partners.
  • · Good knowledge of infrastructure, systems, networks and security issues.
  • · Leadership, sense of service and ability to organize, prioritize and arbitrate.
  • · Knowledge of the hospital environment is an asset.

Benefits

  • · A newly created position at the heart of the modernization of the Information Technology Service.
  • · Working conditions governed by the collective agreement of the Jura Hospital.
  • · A stimulating working environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • · A cafeteria restaurant at preferential rates.
