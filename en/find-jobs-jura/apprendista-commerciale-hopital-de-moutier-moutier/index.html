Full description

We offer a varied and interesting activity, quality professional supervision, within a dynamic and constantly developing establishment. During your apprenticeship, you will discover various administrative services such as our reception, the billing department, accounting and the human resources department. You will develop diverse skills within a stimulating professional environment!

  • Compulsory education completed or under completion
  • Interest in administrative activities
  • Organizational and Responsibilities
  • Motivation, dynamism and team spirit

We are looking for a curious, reliable and motivated person who likes to learn, communicate and work with others.

During your apprenticeship, you will have the opportunity to evolve within different administrative departments of the Arc Network and you will be able to discover different fields, develop your autonomy and build your professional experience gradually.

We offer you

  • Comprehensive and diversified training
  • Quality professional guidance
  • A modern and dynamic working environment
  • A rewarding experience with a major regional health actor
