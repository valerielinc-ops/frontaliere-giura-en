Full description

Reference code: JR134153

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

We offer a six-month internship opportunity, starting in February 2027, within our R&I Materials team. As an intern, you will play a key role in optimizing the reliability of metallic additive manufacturing technologies without fusion. Your mission will be to develop and integrate advanced skills in alternative processes with conventional production methods, directly impacting the jewellery and watch components of our Houses.

LIABILITY

In close collaboration with the Materials team and under the responsibility of your internship tutor, your missions will be:

  • Critically explore and evaluate innovative powder metallurgy solutions specifically adapted to the requirements of excellence and aesthetics of the luxury industry.
  • Design, plan and execute experimental plans to identify key parameters of additive manufacturing processes.
  • Actively participate in process optimization, from validation of technical feasibility to establishment of robust operating conditions.
  • Conduct detailed microstructural analyses.

PROFILE

  • Student in materials science, Master level with knowledge in metallurgy and additive manufacturing processes.
  • Family.ère with microstructural characterization techniques.
  • Curious, autonomous, proactive.ve.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience within a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Glovelier in the premises of the Swiss Metallurgy Workshops, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
