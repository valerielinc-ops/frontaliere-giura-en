Full description

This position is intended for a physician wishing to engage in a diverse activity, close to the field and at the heart of regional health issues. You will contribute to the proper functioning and development of the medical activities of the Réseau de l'Arc, in particular through:

  • One-time replacements within our Medicentres to ensure continuity of care and support medical teams.
  • Participation in medical and cross-cutting activities carried out by the Medical Directorate.
  • The development and continuous improvement of the care offer of the Arc Network.
  • Contribution to various medical and organizational projects in collaboration with senior physicians and network teams.
  • Support for various cross-cutting activities and projects within our Mental Health Department.
  • Participation in initiatives that meet the health needs of the population of Bernese Jura and Seeland.

This function provides a comprehensive vision of the regional health system and allows interaction with many medical and institutional partners.

  • Federal degree of doctor or recognized foreign diploma.
  • MEBEKO recognition obtained or in progress.
  • Excellent mastery of French, minimum B2 level required.
  • Driver's licence and personal vehicle essential.
  • Good knowledge of the region or real willingness to integrate in a sustainable way.
  • Experience in outpatient, hospital or first resort medicine as an asset.
  • High interest in regional health issues and community medicine.

Above all, we are looking for a doctor with a strong generalist background, able to evolve in different contexts and to intervene with different partners in the network. Curious, flexible and equipped with excellent adaptability, you like to get out of your comfort zone, meet new challenges and contribute to diverse projects. A true generalist in your approach, you can easily navigate between the medical, organizational and relational dimensions of your function. Field doctor, versatile, agile and oriented solutions, you appreciate the diversity of situations, patients and interlocutors. You show autonomy, initiative and an excellent spirit of collaboration.

We propose a varied and stimulating activity within a growing organization, deeply rooted in its territory and population.

You benefit from

  • A modern and interdisciplinary working environment.
  • A rich and diversified practice in first resort medicine.
  • High quality medical infrastructure.
  • An innovative professional framework integrated into the VIVA model.
  • Attractive and evolving working conditions.
  • An organization that values diversity, equity and inclusion.

Do you recognize yourself? You love diversity, challenges and human contacts. You appreciate both clinical activity and projects to develop care for the benefit of the population. You are looking for a function that goes beyond the traditional framework while remaining deeply rooted in the reality of the field. Join the Arc Network and become a truly versatile doctor, committed and involved in health development in our region. Feel free to send us your file, we look forward to your interest!

Community-based institutions with multidisciplinary expertise in patient service, Moutier Hospital and St.Imier Hospital offer quality services in the somatic and mental health fields. With nearly 1'200 employees, the hospitals and Medicentres of the Réseau de l'Arc provide community care to a population of more than 50,000 inhabitants. As part of the development of our medical activities, we are looking for a Medical Officer of Clinic to join our Medical Directorate.
