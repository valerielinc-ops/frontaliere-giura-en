Full description

Reference code: JR134493

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Through your active participation, you will lead and complete digital transformation projects in line with strategic objectives by ensuring that the scope, budget, timelines and optimal allocation of resources are respected.

You will be a key player in accompaniment to change by supporting teams in the deployment of projects to ensure the adoption and sustainability of new uses, but also by involving and bringing together collaborators in the definition and improvement of solutions.

Your proposal strength will greatly contribute to the development and structuring of digital transformation while carrying out optimization initiatives.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

Having a degree in engineering or equivalent, you have an experience in a similar position ideally in the field of watchmaking, automotive or pharmaceutical industry.

You have a special appeal for technological and digital intelligence as well as for project management and the "field" approach.

Your good relationship and communication skills allow you to easily popularize topics to facilitate understanding and to be an excellent facilitator to accompany teams in the culture of change.

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

We offer flexible working hours in a varied and stimulating environment. We benefit from the benefits of the collective labour agreement of the Swiss watch and microtech industries, including 5 weeks of holidays per year plus additional leave related to catching up.

Due to our environmental impact, we focus on soft mobility and public transport with financial participation. Our restaurant offers fresh and varied meals every day at a great price. Oriented towards well-being at work, our event committee organizes many cultural and sporting activities throughout the year.

In addition, the Richemont Group has been certified Equal-Pay, which ensures fair remuneration for all our employees. Not to mention our Richemont pension fund, which offers good benefits allowing every employee to ensure their financial future related to social benefits.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials. · We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity · We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity · We take care of the world we live in

YOUR TRAVEL WITH US

If your profile matches our search, we are waiting for your application. You will first be contacted by Human Resources for an exploratory call. Subsequently, a first interview with the Supply Chain Manager and Human Resources will be offered, followed by a second interview with other line workers.
