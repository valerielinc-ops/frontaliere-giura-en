Role overview

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation.

Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

The Stainless Steel Shear & Fabrication Operator is responsible for operating manual shearing equipment and other fabrication machinery to process stainless steel components according to engineering drawings and production requirements.

Main responsibilities

  • Essential Duties and Responsibilities
  • Set up and operate manual shearing equipment to cut stainless steel materials to specified dimensions and tolerances.
  • Read and interpret engineering drawings, blueprints, work orders, and manufacturing specifications.
  • Perform layout, measuring, and marking of stainless steel materials.
  • Operate fabrication equipment, including but not limited to: Roll forming and plate rolling machines Drill presses Band saws and cold saws Angle grinders Deburring equipment Other fabrication machinery as assigned
  • Verify dimensions and quality using measuring tools such as tape measures, calipers, squares, and gauges.
  • Complete fabrication operations accurately while meeting production schedules and quality standards.
  • Safely move and handle stainless steel materials and fabricated parts throughout the production process.
  • Maintain equipment and work areas in a clean and organized condition.
  • Follow all company safety procedures and wear required personal protective equipment (PPE).

Key requirements

  • Required
  • High school diploma or GED.
  • Manufacturing, metal fabrication, or sheet metal experience preferred.
  • Ability to read and interpret blueprints, engineering drawings, and shop work instructions.
  • Strong manufacturing math skills, including: Fractions and decimals Measurement conversions Basic geometry Accurate tape measure reading
  • Experience using common measuring and inspection tools.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality workmanship.
  • Mechanical aptitude and ability to operate fabrication equipment safely.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Physical Requirements
  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods throughout the workday.
  • Frequently lift, carry, push, and pull materials weighing up to 50 pounds.
  • Perform repetitive hand and arm movements associated with fabrication operations.
