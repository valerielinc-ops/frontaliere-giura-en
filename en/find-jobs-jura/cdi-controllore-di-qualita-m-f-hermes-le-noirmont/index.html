Quality Controller (M/F)

Main place of work: Brügg (BE) Secondary place of work: Le Noirmont (JU)

As part of its function, the Quality Controller is the guarantor of the quality of Hermès products. Based within the Quality Control Department, he/she is responsible for the technical, functional and aesthetic control of watches and watch heads (TDM), strictly applying the criteria defined in the framework of Hermès Horloger's Quality Policy.

It validates product compliance prior to release and actively contributes to the detection, analysis and treatment of anomalies, in order to ensure customer satisfaction and the excellence of marketed products. He/she reports to the Quality Control Manager and works closely with Quality Assurance, Development and Production.

Your main tasks will be

Quality control

You will carry out the technical, functional and aesthetic control of watches and TDM in accordance with the Quality standards of the House, and will check the proper functioning of the complications and the different features of the products.

You will validate the conformity of the products before their release, and will intervene by bringing your quality expertise during problems encountered in production or during specific controls.

You will be involved in the validation of bids, preseries or first manufactures when required, and will perform analyses related to reclothing, customer returns and market returns.

Finally, you will monitor the quality performance observed during the final check and propose the associated actions.

Management of non-compliance

You will ensure the recovery of all anomalies detected during the checks, and identify, document and qualify the observed non-compliances.

You will manage and communicate these non-compliances to internal and external partners, participate in cause analyses and follow-up of corrective action plans, and contribute to securing flows in case of quality drift.

Transverse communication and support

You will collaborate with the Production, Development and QA teams to ensure the effective treatment of quality issues, and participate in quality meetings by sharing the findings.

You will accompany subcontractors in understanding quality requirements when necessary, and will be a reference player on quality standards of final control.

Specific activities

You will perform specific controls related to certain models or complications, and ensure the packaging of products when required by the current standards.

You will be able to propose to improve the quality of the product, the control methods and the efficiency of the department, and will participate in the optimization of the workflows, workstations and control tools.

Finally, you will contribute to the continuous improvement projects led by the Quality Department, as well as to the definition and evolution of the quality standards of final control.

Your profile

You justify prior experience in production or quality in the watch industry.

An experience in final watch control or watchmaking dressing is a significant advantage.

You have good mastery of reading plans and interpretation of quality repositories, as well as knowledge of conventional computer tools such as Word/Excel, Outlook, Quickcontrol / Ellistat and M3.

You have excellent visual acuity and a great sense of detail, as well as rigour and a great capacity for concentration.

You have a sense of observation and confidentiality, while showing a good relationship and enjoying teamwork.

Finally, you have an excellent understanding and expression, both oral and written, of French.
