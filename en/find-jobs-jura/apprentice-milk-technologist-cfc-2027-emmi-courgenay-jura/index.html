Full description

Are you curious about how you make your favorite cheese or your favorite yogurt? At Emmi, you dive into the fascinating world of dairy technology. Here, you don't just taste dairy products – you learn to make them from A to Z.

What you can do

  • You learn how to turn milk into a wide variety of products – you manage the stages of production, filling, refining, packaging and even tasting.
  • You set up, monitor and clean state-of-the-art production facilities, always respecting hygiene standards.
  • You analyze the products in the laboratory – chemically and microbiologically – in order to guarantee an irreproachable quality.
  • You work in different departments and on several production sites, which allows you to understand the profession as a whole.
  • You discover how a simple milk becomes a finished product ready to be tasted – and you actively contribute to each step.
  • Thanks to your commitment, you ensure that our products arrive fresh and in perfect condition to consumers.

What you bring

  • You finished compulsory school with a good level in French, mathematics and natural sciences.
  • You like dairy products and you have a good sense of smell and taste.
  • You're comfortable with the technique and you like to understand how machines work.
  • You pay attention to hygiene, you work with care and you always keep a good overview.
  • You live in the area where the cheese factory is and you have a reasonable way to get to work.

What We Offer

  • Additional salary and optional participation in the results: You receive a 13th month's salary and participation in the company's results.
  • 6 weeks of vacation: More time for rest, travel and leisure.
  • Excellent prospects for the future: 80% of our apprentices benefit from a fixed employment contract at Emmi at the end of their training.
  • Cost support to focus more on your learning: We pay for textbooks, SBB half-rate subscription, work clothes and support you up to CHF 400.– to purchase your school laptop.
  • Fun and team spirit: Rejoice each year for an unforgettable trip to Europapark. We also organise a day of joint integration and a common camp for apprentices at the end of the first year of apprenticeship.
  • Products Free Emmi: Enjoy our delicious Emmi products during your work and after work. With discounts of up to 20% in our stores, you will never have to take away your favorite items.
