About SRAL Carrier Management Team

The SRAL Carrier Management team comprises approximately 28 finance professionals across the region and operates within the broader Finance function globally within the GFO&T division.

The team collaborates closely with local, regional, and global stakeholders to deliver timely, accurate, and reliable financial reporting, meeting both regulatory and statutory requirements as well as internal analytical needs.

Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities Financial & Regulatory Reporting
  • Support the preparation and submission of financial and MAS regulatory reports for Singapore entities.
  • Assist in the preparation of statutory financial statements and/or regulatory returns in accordance with local requirements and internal policies.
  • Perform financial closing reconciliations and investigate variances and exceptions.
  • Ensure reporting deliverables are completed accurately and within established timelines. Financial Close Activities
  • Support quarter-end and year-end close activities, including but not limited to intercompany confirmations and settlements, other relevant statistical and regulatory submissions/surveys.
  • Prepare journal entries, supporting schedules and working papers.
  • Maintain appropriate documentation for reporting and audit purposes. Controls & Compliance
  • Follow established financial reporting controls and procedures.
  • Support internal and external audit requests by preparing required documentation and analyses.

Key requirements

  • & Experience
  • Bachelor's degree in accounting, Finance or a related discipline.

  • Experience within insurance, reinsurance or financial services is preferred but not essential.
  • Knowledge of IFRS and regulatory reporting concepts is desirable. Skills & Competencies
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.

  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet reporting deadlines.
