Full description

Reference code: JR134035

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Within the HR team, you play a key role in the operational and administrative support of our teams. Versatile and rigorous, you intervene throughout the life cycle of the employee, from recruitment to training, while ensuring compliance and administrative excellence in our department.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Recruitment & Integration

  • Manage the entire recruitment process for temporary production positions.
  • Ensure the integration of new employees and monitor their trial periods.

Personnel Administration & Partner Tracking

  • Administer employee files and verify documentation required for new hires.
  • Coordinate and monitor temporary staff files in close collaboration with our partner employment agencies.
  • Organize and manage the physical and/or digital archiving of collaborating folders.

HR Projects & Compliance

  • Actively participate in the redesign and updating of function descriptions.
  • Monitor the deployment and completion of compliance modules by all employees.

Training

  • To provide administrative support for the management and organisation of internal and external training.

HOW ARE YOU GONNA USE US?

Holder of a SWC of commercial employee, ideally supplemented by the HR certificate, you justify a successful experience of 2 to 3 years in a similar function. You master the basics of Swiss labour law and ideally have a good knowledge of the CTC of the Swiss watch industry and microtechnics. Perfectly comfortable with IT tools and HRIS, you are recognized for your rigor, your sense of organization and your total discretion.

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

We offer flexible working hours in a varied and stimulating environment. We benefit from the benefits of the collective labour agreement of the Swiss watch and microtechnic industries, including 5 weeks of holidays per year, plus additional leave related to catching up.

Due to our environmental impact, we focus on soft mobility and public transport with financial participation. Our restaurant offers fresh and varied meals every day at a great price. Oriented towards well-being at work, our event committee offers many cultural and sporting activities throughout the year.

In addition, the Richemont Group has been certified Equal-Pay, ensuring fair remuneration for all our employees. Not to mention our Richemont pension fund, which offers good benefits allowing every employee to ensure their financial future related to social benefits.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity
  • We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity
  • We take care of the world we live in

YOUR TRAVEL WITH US

If your profile matches our search, you will be contacted for an exploratory call. Subsequently, you will be offered a first interview and then a second interview with other hierarchical stakeholders.
